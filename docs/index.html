Good to know

The model is XGBoost, trained on 3,591 district-seasons (1981–2024). Scores come from testing on 2016–2024, years it had not seen during training. It explains differences between places and weather well, but tends to under-predict the newest high-yield years, because tree models cannot predict beyond what they have seen. Predictions show patterns in historical data, not guaranteed outcomes. Irrigation, seeds and farming practice also matter.
